
Notebook 04 - EDA Findings

- EDA was performed using the training split only.
- The target is imbalanced; late orders are the minority class.
- Missing values occur in several features and require handling during feature engineering.
- Numerical variables show different ranges, skewness, and outliers.
- Categorical variables vary considerably in cardinality; rare categories may require grouping.
- Late-delivery rates vary across time and geographic groups.
- Purchase-time and estimated-delivery information are potential predictive features.
- Customer ZIP/state and seller geography may provide useful delivery information.
- Customer coordinates are currently unavailable, so customer-seller distance could not yet be calculated.
- Post-delivery information, reviews, actual delivery dates, IDs, and the target itself must not be used as predictors.
